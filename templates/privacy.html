Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

When using flatten.site, we process your files temporarily for the purpose of flattening hierarchies. We do not store any uploaded files or their contents beyond the processing duration.

2. How We Use Your Information

The files you upload are used solely for the purpose of processing and generating flattened hierarchies. All files are automatically deleted after processing is complete.

3. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to maintain the security of your data during processing. All file processing is done server-side and files are removed immediately after processing.

4. Cookies

flatten.site uses only essential cookies necessary for the proper functioning of the service. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

5. Third-Party Services

We do not share any information with third parties. The service operates independently without relying on external services for file processing.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our contact form.
